Advocacy

Unethical technology often starts in research and then gets developed and sold by companies. Misuse becomes possible only if that complex technological chain disregards ethics and accountability. We push back where norms are set: companies whose products are misused, journals, and publishers. We work with journalists to disseminate our findings or comment on their investigations.
